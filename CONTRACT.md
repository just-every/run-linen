# Linen proposed product guarantees

Planning contract, 27 September 2026. These are requirements for future work,
not claims of implemented behavior. Evidence gates are in [ACCEPTANCE.md](ACCEPTANCE.md).
E1–E7 preserve all seven workflows and exceptions from the 22 September ITSM
research decision. Cross-cutting
clauses apply from the first connected proof; the entire floor is required for
the complete employee release. O1–O3 add flagship obligations without replacing it.

## Complete employee service desk

| ID | Required useful behavior, including exceptions |
|---|---|
| **E1 Help and incidents** | Authenticated portal/email intake, stable conversation, incident/request classification, accountable routing and reassignment, employee replies/private notes, attachments, impact, service/device context, response/resolution commitments and restoration evidence. Employees can correct, confirm when useful, report “still not working” or reopen; do not require confirmation when fresh independent evidence proves usable work. Handle duplicate messages, unknown sender, attachment failure, wrong classification, absent assignee, holidays/timezones, nonresponse and simultaneous edits. Closure policy is disclosed; closure is distinct from verified recovery. |
| **E2 Access, device and software requests** | Accept a natural-language request or optional catalog selection; resolve beneficiary, purpose and prerequisites from permitted context, then show exact authority, fulfilment owner, provision/delivery evidence and comprehensible rejection/completion. Ask only for a genuinely missing fact or decision. Missing managers enter an accountable exception; substitutes require actual delegated authority. Material changes invalidate affected approval. Handle partial fulfilment, no stock/licence, cancellation after effects, failed/unknown provider actions, temporary access expiry and independently verified revocation; do not require routine reapproval or confirmation when standing authority and machine evidence suffice. |
| **E3 Join, move and leave** | Authorised HR/manager initiation with effective times, task dependencies, accounts, access and devices; an authoritative HR event may start routine work without a separate employee/IT request. Keep the distinct obligations: join prepares the authorised baseline and physical fulfilment; move compares retained, added and removed access; leave revokes access and preserves separate custody/return duties. Reconcile every obligation. Handle changed dates, cancelled hires, contractor expiry, urgent termination, already-disabled identity, partial revocation, unreturned laptop and ambiguous local times/daylight saving. HR remains authoritative for employment facts; completed subtasks cannot conceal outstanding security/custody work. |
| **E4 Custody and context** | Stable device ID/serial, person, location, lifecycle, receipt/return and service ownership linked to work. Preserve source and freshness. Reconcile conflicting serials, reassignment in progress, missing devices, disposed-but-assigned records, renamed/merged/deactivated people and stale MDM feeds. Imported observation is distinct from verified custody. |
| **E5 Shared incidents and problems** | Accountable shared interruption, impact, linked employee incidents, update cadence, recurring cause/workaround and follow-up. Linen correlates candidate reports from permitted evidence and preserves separate employee work; people can correct mistaken links without manual triage as the default. Parent recovery does not establish every employee's recovery. Preserve confidential scopes, show stale workarounds and failed notifications, and retain separate restoration and cause/prevention outcomes. |
| **E6 Controlled changes** | Service, owner, risk/impact, dependencies, scheduled window, versioned authorisation, exact implementation, execution and rollback/compensation evidence, success/failure and follow-up. Standard low-risk changes can use standing grants. Handle emergency authority, conflicts, post-approval edits, partial execution, failed rollback and post-change incidents. A change record is not authority in a provider. |
| **E7 Knowledge and management review** | Scoped reviewed articles, explicit expiry, search and escalation into a request with context. Reports cover age, response/resolution commitments, blocked approvals, lifecycle obligations, recurrence and outcomes with definitions, range and timezone. Paused clocks, changed schedules, reopened work, permission changes and failed refreshes remain explicit; closure counts never substitute for fulfilment. |

## Authority, evidence and operation

- **A1 Identity and privacy.** Authenticate scoped principals at every private
  boundary. Separate requester/beneficiary, approver, HR, service operator, owner,
  auditor and integration rights. Enforce current scope on records, attachments,
  search, notifications, exports and AI context. Protect restricted departures and
  incidents; minimise HR data; support retention and authorised erasure without
  restoring erased content on replay. Audit privileged read/export and policy changes.
- **A2 Granted action.** Versioned grants bind action, target, beneficiary,
  conditions, limits and time. Standing low-risk authority avoids repeated human
  approvals. Wider-impact action needs appropriate authority. At-action checks
  reject stale, expired, revoked or concurrently superseded decisions. Retrieved
  text and model output never confer authority. Source/policy editing cannot
  silently let the executor expand its own privileges.
- **A3 Durable effects.** Persist intent and operation identity before dispatch;
  fence competing execution and human takeover. Reconcile unknown remote effects
  before repeat. Resume after crash without duplicating confirmed effects. Record
  partial completion, failed compensation and remaining obligations. Revocation
  or cancellation request is not proof that a remote action stopped.
- **A4 Verifiable outcomes.** Bind each claim to fresh attributed evidence,
  target/cohort and verifier version. Distinguish submission, effect and usable
  recovery. Retain unknown/disputed results and negative checks; no false success
  from sent instructions, tool status, nonresponse or a model's self-assessment.
- **A5 Legible service.** Employees see truthful status and next action in their
  conversation; operators see only accountable exceptions in their work context
  and can take over. Linen owns permitted routing, safe follow-up and escalation;
  routine acknowledgement is not a gate to machine-verifiable completion. Preserve
  accessible desktop/mobile paths, private/public separation, real delivery states,
  calendars/escalation and an audited outage recovery-access procedure.
- **A6 Data and source custody.** Imports preserve identities, relationships,
  public/private history, attachments and meaningful status/approval history;
  imported approval is history, not new authority. Reconcile dry run and actual
  cutover, late arrivals and dual execution. Usable export/restore preserves policy,
  evidence and intent. Updates preserve applied migration history and test existing
  data and owner adaptations. Source may diverge deeply without losing eligibility
  for useful upstream improvements.
- **A7 Honest operating and cost boundaries.** Declare actual supported commands,
  runtime, resources, provider effects and recovery targets. Record every model/tool
  attempt, failures and uncertain charges, budgets, latency and all human work.
  Count touches and active minutes for account sign-ins/consents, setup gaps,
  corrections, physical custody, vendor/technician tasks, takeover and recovery;
  report unresolved and missed work beside any lower-interruption result. Local/
  fixture, real provider, hosted and owner evidence stay distinct. Missing
  configuration cannot produce mock data or successful fallback execution.

## Flagship service operations

- **O1 Service graph and major incidents.** Relate business impact, services,
  technical dependencies, people and suppliers with provenance, freshness and
  uncertainty. Coordinate incident command, parallel scoped diagnostics, escalation,
  audience-specific updates and recovery checks across affected cohorts. Integrate
  actual monitoring/on-call systems under declared authority; do not infer health
  or dependencies from missing data.
- **O2 Change, release and prevention.** Coordinate change windows, dependencies,
  deployment gates, canaries and compensations with real provider evidence. Connect
  known errors, recurrence and post-incident learning to owned preventive changes.
  Measure sustained recovery and effectiveness; a drafted postmortem or prevention
  suggestion is not a completed improvement.
- **O3 Lifecycle and economics.** Extend custody through acquisition, assignment,
  renewal, repair, return and disposal; manage software entitlements and reclamation
  with service impact and authority. Track supplier commitments, handoffs and breaches.
  Show attributed service costs, demand and avoided-work hypotheses with uncertainty.
  Owners' agents can add integrations and deeply adapt source through the tested
  change lane, retaining authority and verification guarantees.

Linen does not become the HR/payroll master, IdP, endpoint-management product,
observability store or procurement ledger merely by integrating one. Support
those systems' real selected effects; do not claim universal provider compatibility.
These boundaries preserve a complete IT service product without absorbing every
adjacent application.

## Frontier extension obligations

[F1–F7](FRONTIER-EXTENSION.md#seven-extensions-that-change-the-product) extend the
flagship destination with persistent service commitments, semantic observation,
unfamiliar causal research, autonomous service engineering, executable models,
tested reusable capabilities and continuous service simplification. They inherit
A1–A7 and preserve E1–E7 and O1–O3 verbatim. Their early demonstration is additional
connected evidence, not a substitute for any complete employee release clause.
The [frontier evidence gates](ACCEPTANCE.md#frontier-extension-evidence) govern
claims of this capability; all remain unimplemented planning requirements.
