**People & permissions** lists everyone in your workspace and what each may do. Open it from
your profile menu.

## Add someone

The owner adds people with **Add a workspace member**:

1. Their **Name** and **Work email**.
2. An **Initial password** of at least 12 characters. Share it privately; they should change it.
3. Their **Granted roles**, one per line.

They then sign in at `/app` with that email and password.

## Roles

| Role | What it is for |
|---|---|
| `owner` | Runs the installation: connections, people, grants, backups. |
| `operator` | The service team: works requests, private notes, service controls. |
| `employee` | Asks for help and follows their own conversations. |
| `approver` | Decides catalog requests and choices that name them. |
| `hr` | Records joining, moving and leaving, and sees restricted HR conversations. |
| `auditor` | Reads records and history without changing them. |
| `integration` | A non-interactive identity for connected systems. |

A role is the start, not the whole answer: each record's own scope and the grants you issue
narrow what anyone may do.

## Operations and recovery

**Operations & recovery** in the profile menu shows everything Linen has started, its state and
any uncertainty left over. From there the service team can check a remote effect, verify an
outcome independently, take over an operation or cancel work that has not started.
