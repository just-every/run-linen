Joining, moving and leaving each keep their own steps. Access and devices stay separate until
each one is done, so a laptop that has not come back never hides behind access that has.

Open them from **A little more context** on **Your work**: **A person joining, moving or
leaving** and **Devices & custody**. Both are for the service team and HR.

## Record a change

1. In **A person joining, moving or leaving**, open **Record an authoritative HR change**.
2. Choose the person, what is changing (joining, moving or leaving) and the effective date and
   time.
3. Name the HR source and its revision, the accountable owner, and the access to add, keep or
   remove. Mark an urgent departure when it is one.
4. Save. Linen lists each responsibility under **Outstanding and completed responsibilities**:
   access granted or removed, devices delivered or returned, each with its own owner and state.

You can **Change the effective date** or **Cancel this lifecycle change** later; both are
recorded.

## Devices and custody

**Devices & custody** keeps where things stand:

- **Record a device** adds a laptop, phone or other asset.
- **Record a custody change** moves it to a person or back to the service team.
- **Record the physical handoff** closes a delivery or a return once it has really happened.

A device delivery shows **Next owner** until the handoff is recorded. Linen never marks a
physical handoff as done on its own.
