Every request in Linen is one conversation. People ask in their own words; there is no
category to pick and no approver to find. The same conversation then shows what Linen did,
what is ready and what still needs someone.

## Ask

Open **Your work**. Under **What would make work easier?** write what you need, the way you
would say it to a colleague:

> I need Orders access for the west-region cover this week.

Attach a file with **Attach a file** if it helps, then choose **Send**. Linen saves your message
first, so nothing is lost if your connection drops, and opens the conversation.

If you would rather pick from a list, **Or choose a known service** under the conversation
offers your service catalogue. It keeps the same request; it does not start a second one.

## Follow it

The conversation shows, from the top:

- **The request** and its state: open, paused, closed or cancelled.
- **Progress**: one note for each responsibility, what happened and who owns the next step.
  Work that independent evidence has verified says so, and needs no acknowledgement from you.
- **What's ready, and what remains**: each responsibility with its date and state. Temporary
  access shows its removal as a separate line, scheduled for the expiry you asked for.
- **Keep the conversation going**: add a detail, change a date, or say what is still not
  working.
- **What happened**: every message, in order.

Your earlier conversations are listed under **Your conversations** on **Your work**.

## When it is not right

- **Still not working?** reopens the conversation and records what is still in your way.
- **Cancel this request** records your cancellation. It does not claim that something already
  done has been undone; anything uncertain stays visible until it is reconciled.

## For the service team

People with service-team access (owners, operators and HR) also see:

- **Private note** beside **Shared reply** in the composer. A private note is hidden from
  employees without service-team access.
- **Responsibilities needing you** on **Your work**, listing the requests that wait on the team.
- **Service controls** at the foot of each conversation: assign, pause or resume, and close.
  Closing a conversation is not evidence that the work was restored.
