# Adopted defaults awaiting a ct ruling

Defaults a build round adopted where no ct ruling existed yet, moved here from CONTRACT.md in the 28 October 2026 contract cleanup. Each stays in force until ct rules; a ct ruling goes to RULINGS.md. The adopted defaults that ct's 28 October ruling pass decided are now RL-101 to RL-108.

## Branding and devices (CHANNEL-004)

*Branding and devices semantic (adopted default, pending ct approval).* Only an owner sets branding; the portal name is plain text, colors are checked for readable contrast, the logo is a PNG, JPEG or WebP of at most 150 KB (never SVG), and "My devices" lists only the member's own devices through a reviewed link.
